How do I list my site?

Tap the + button, sign in (Apple, Google, or email), then tap + again to add a listing. Listing is free.

How do I edit or delete my listing?

Open Advertise → My listings. Each listing has Edit and delete buttons.

What is a Sponsored placement?

Sponsored listings get the large spotlight cards at the top of the feed for 1 to 30 days. You buy them from My listings with an In-App Purchase. "Featured" is separate: it is earned automatically by listings with great ratings and lots of visits.

How do I report a listing or a review?

Tap the ... menu on a listing or review and choose Report. We review reports and remove content that breaks our rules.

How do I hide a listing or block an advertiser?

Use the ... menu on a listing and choose Hide this listing or Block this advertiser. You can undo these under the Style menu → Hidden and blocked.

How do I delete my account?

Advertise → My listings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, listings, and reviews.

A purchase didn't apply to my listing

Reopen the app, which retries unfinished purchases automatically. If it still doesn't show, email us with your listing's title and the date of the purchase.
