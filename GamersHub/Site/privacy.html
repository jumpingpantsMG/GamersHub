Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

GamersHub ("we", "us") is an app that lets people advertise gaming websites and lets other people browse and review them. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It is operated by Gamer's hub.

What we collect

If you advertise (create an account):

  • Your email address and a password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form), or the account identifier and email that Apple or Google share with us if you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google.
  • The listings you create: title, description, website link, cover image link, category, and age rating.
  • Purchase records for sponsored placements: Apple's transaction ID, the product bought, and which listing it applies to. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

If you only browse: you don't need an account. We count how many times a listing is viewed or visited. These are totals per listing and are not tied to you.

If you write a review: the app creates a random anonymous ID on your device so you can leave one review per listing. We store your rating, your comment, and that anonymous ID. We do not ask for your name or email.

If you report something: we store the report, the reason, and any note you add.

Kept only on your device: your theme and style settings, and the listings and people you hide or block. These are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show listings, ratings, and reviews, and let advertisers manage their listings.
  • To process and apply sponsored placements.
  • To review reports and remove content that breaks our rules or the law.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Apple handles In-App Purchases and Sign in with Apple.
  • Google handles Sign in with Google if you choose it.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.

Third-party sites and images

Listings link to websites run by other people. When you tap Visit, the site opens in your browser and is governed by that site's own privacy policy. Cover images are loaded from addresses the advertiser provides, so the host of an image can see your IP address when it loads.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: Advertise → My listings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your listings, and your reviews. Purchase records may be kept without any link to you, as needed for accounting and legal reasons. Reviews left through an anonymous device ID can be removed by contacting us.

Children

GamersHub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Advertisers choose an age rating for each listing, and you can hide any listing you don't want to see. Please report anything inappropriate from inside the app.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can delete your account in the app, or contact us and we'll help.

Security

We use reputable hosting and sign-in providers and limit who can access stored data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
